Process

From a sentence to a clearance list

01
Describe the business
Type or speak a plain-language description — district, sector, investment, headcount, whatever you know. No form-filling required to start.
02
NLP extraction
A rule-based extractor pulls investment, employee count, district, sector and hazard flags out of the free text, with unit-aware parsing for power, land and water.
03
Gaps get flagged, not guessed
Anything the extractor couldn't find stays visibly blank and is called out by voice — in English, Hindi or Punjabi — instead of silently defaulting.
04
ANN forward pass
The completed profile is preprocessed and pushed through a trained neural network that scores twelve licenses, NOCs and registrations independently.
05
Dependency-ordered journey
Required approvals are arranged into the realistic sequence a business would pursue them in — not just a flat list of yes/no results.

Under the hood

The Approval Intelligence Engine

A feed-forward neural network trained on Punjab industrial-approval records, scoring twelve licenses, NOCs and registrations as independent probabilities rather than one combined verdict.

Architecture
Feed-forward ANN
Output heads
12 approvals
Framework
TensorFlow / Keras
Preprocessing
scikit-learn pipeline
Layer shape
Input→ 256→ 128→ 64→ Output · 12

Each output neuron is scored independently and thresholded at 50% confidence, then the required set is re-ordered into the dependency-aware sequence shown in your journey — building-plan approval before a fire NOC, a fire NOC before an environmental clearance, and so on.
