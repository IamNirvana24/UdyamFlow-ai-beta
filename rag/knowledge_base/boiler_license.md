# Boiler Registration and Certificate
slug: boiler_license
authority: Directorate of Boilers, Punjab (Chief Inspector of Boilers), under the Boilers Act, 1923
applies_to: Any premises operating a steam boiler above the prescribed capacity, and economisers connected to such boilers.

## What it is
Registration of the boiler and issue of a certificate permitting it to be operated at a specified maximum pressure, following inspection by the Inspector of Boilers.

## Why it is required
A boiler is a pressure vessel and a failure is catastrophic. Operating an unregistered boiler is an offence, voids insurance, and makes the occupier personally liable. The certificate specifies the maximum permissible working pressure, and operating above it is itself a violation.

## Typical documents required
- Boiler manufacturer's drawings and construction certificates
- Material test certificates for the pressure parts
- Details of mountings, fittings, and safety valves
- Boiler house layout and foundation drawing
- Details of the fuel used
- Certificate of the boiler attendant or operator, who must hold a valid competency certificate
- Previous inspection reports where the boiler is second-hand

## Position in the approval sequence
Applies only where the process actually uses a steam boiler — common in food processing, textiles, paper, and chemicals, absent in most assembly and light engineering units. Sits late in the sequence, after installation and before production.

## Practical notes
Certificates are renewed after periodic inspection, typically annually, and the boiler must be shut and opened up for internal inspection at renewal. Only a person holding a valid boiler attendant certificate may be in charge of the boiler in operation, which is a staffing constraint as much as a licensing one.
