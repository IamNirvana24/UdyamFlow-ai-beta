# Approval Sequence and Dependencies
slug: approval_sequence
authority: Cross-cutting — this document explains how the twelve approvals relate to each other in time
applies_to: Any entrepreneur planning the order in which to file applications.

## The recommended order
UdyamFlow orders approvals by dependency rather than by department:

1. **Building Plan Approval** — first, because land use and sanctioned drawings gate everything physical
2. **Trade License** — establishes the business at a legitimate address
3. **GST Registration** — needed before any compliant invoice can be raised
4. **Labour Registrations** — shops and establishments early; EPF and ESI the moment headcount thresholds are crossed
5. **Factory License** — requires approved plans and a built premises
6. **Fire NOC** — plan stage runs with building approval, final NOC after construction
7. **Pollution NOC** — Consent to Establish very early, Consent to Operate near the end
8. **Environmental Clearance** — only for scheduled projects, and it precedes construction entirely
9. **Electricity NOC** — applied mid-sequence because release takes weeks
10. **Boiler License** — only where a steam boiler is installed, late in the sequence
11. **Explosives License** — only above storage thresholds, site NOC raised early
12. **FSSAI** — food sector only, late, once layout and equipment are final

## What genuinely blocks what
- Without Building Plan Approval there is no occupancy certificate, and without that the Factory Licence and often the power connection stall.
- Environmental Clearance, where it applies, must precede both construction and the Pollution Board's Consent to Establish.
- Consent to Establish precedes construction; Consent to Operate precedes commercial production.
- Fire NOC plan approval shapes the building design itself, so it cannot be left until after construction.
- The Electrical Inspector's approval precedes energising a high tension installation.

## What runs in parallel
GST registration, labour registrations, and trade licence involve different departments with no dependency on each other and should be filed simultaneously rather than sequentially. Treating the list as strictly serial is the most common scheduling mistake.

## Which approvals take longest
Environmental Clearance is by far the longest, running in months because of baseline monitoring seasons and the public hearing. Pollution Consent for a Red-category unit is next. Electricity connection can be slow where infrastructure work is needed. GST registration and trade licence are comparatively quick.

## Start-here advice
Before anything else, confirm the master plan zoning for the plot and whether the project attracts Environmental Clearance. Both are threshold questions whose answers reshape the entire schedule, and both are cheapest to answer before land is purchased.
