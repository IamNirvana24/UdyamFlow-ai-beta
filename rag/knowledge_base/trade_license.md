# Trade License
slug: trade_license
authority: Municipal Corporation / Municipal Council / Nagar Panchayat (Local Urban Body), Department of Local Government, Punjab
applies_to: Almost every commercial establishment operating within municipal limits, regardless of sector or size.

## What it is
A Trade License is permission from the local urban body to carry out a specified trade or business at a specified premises. It does not certify quality or safety; it confirms the local body knows the business exists, that the trade is permitted in that zone, and that municipal dues are being collected.

## Why it is required
Operating a commercial premises without a trade licence within municipal limits is an offence under the relevant municipal Act. It is also the document most other departments ask for as proof that the business exists at a legitimate address.

## Typical documents required
- Proof of premises ownership or a registered rent/lease agreement
- Identity and address proof of the proprietor, partners, or directors
- Site plan / layout of the premises
- No Objection Certificate from the property owner if premises are rented
- Latest property tax receipt for the premises
- Incorporation or partnership documents where applicable

## Position in the approval sequence
Applied for early, immediately after the premises is secured and building plan approval is in place where construction is involved. Many later approvals reference the trade licence number.

## Practical notes
Renewed annually or as prescribed by the local body. Fees vary by municipality, trade category, and premises area, so the exact amount must be confirmed with the relevant Municipal Corporation. Punjab routes most of these applications through the state single-window portal (Invest Punjab).
