# Labour Registrations
slug: labour_registration
authority: Department of Labour, Government of Punjab; Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) and Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) at the central level
applies_to: Employers crossing prescribed headcount thresholds, which differ between each of the registrations grouped here.

## What it is
A cluster of employment-related registrations rather than a single licence:
- **Shops and Commercial Establishments registration** — for commercial premises, applied for shortly after opening
- **EPF registration** — provident fund, triggered above a prescribed employee headcount
- **ESI registration** — medical and cash benefits, triggered above a prescribed employee headcount
- **Contract Labour registration** — where contract workmen are engaged above the prescribed number
- **Professional tax registration** where applicable

## Why it is required
These are the registrations most often neglected by new units and most often caught in inspection. Liability for arrears runs from the date the threshold was crossed, not the date of registration, so late registration carries backdated dues plus damages and interest.

## Typical documents required
- Incorporation or partnership documents and PAN
- Proof of premises
- List of employees with dates of joining, wages, and identity details
- Bank account details of the establishment
- Digital signature of the authorised signatory
- Details of contractors engaged, for contract labour registration

## Position in the approval sequence
Early to mid-sequence. The Shops and Establishments registration is filed soon after opening; EPF and ESI become due the moment the headcount threshold is crossed, which may be well after opening for a unit that scales up.

## Practical notes
Thresholds differ between EPF and ESI, and ESI applicability is also geographic — it applies in notified areas. The single most common failure is a unit that hires past the threshold during a growth phase and registers only after an inspection.
