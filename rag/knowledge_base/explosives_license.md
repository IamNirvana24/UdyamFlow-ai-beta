# Explosives Licence
slug: explosives_license
authority: Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO), Government of India, with district administration involvement for site NOC
applies_to: Manufacture, possession, storage, sale, transport, or use of explosives, and storage of compressed gases and petroleum products above prescribed quantities.

## What it is
A licence under the Explosives Act and Rules, or the Petroleum Rules / Static and Mobile Pressure Vessels Rules depending on the substance, permitting a stated quantity of a stated substance to be stored or handled at a stated site.

## Why it is required
These substances carry mass-casualty potential. Licensing is quantity-specific and site-specific, and both the substance list and the permitted quantity are conditions of the licence.

## What commonly triggers it in ordinary industry
Most units that need this are not explosives manufacturers. They trigger it by storing industrial gases, solvents, or fuel above threshold quantities — LPG bullets, dissolved acetylene cylinders, diesel storage above the exempt limit, or bulk solvent tanks.

## Typical documents required
- Site plan showing safety distances from the storage to boundaries, buildings, and public roads
- Construction drawings of the storage shed or tank installation
- District Magistrate's No Objection Certificate for the site
- Fire safety arrangements and firefighting equipment details
- Details of the substance, its classification, and the maximum quantity to be stored
- Details of the competent person supervising the installation
- Test certificates for vessels and fittings

## Position in the approval sequence
Late in the sequence, tied to the physical storage installation, but the site NOC stage must be raised early because the safety distance requirements can constrain the site layout itself.

## Practical notes
Safety distance rules are the binding constraint and are non-negotiable — a site that cannot provide the required clearance cannot be licensed at that quantity, regardless of any other compliance. Reducing the stored quantity below the threshold is sometimes a faster route than licensing.
