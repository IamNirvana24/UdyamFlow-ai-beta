# Fire NOC
slug: fire_noc
authority: Punjab Fire Service, operating through the Municipal Corporation / local fire station, under the Punjab Fire Prevention and Fire Safety framework
applies_to: Buildings above prescribed height or covered area, industrial premises, storage of combustible material, and premises with public assembly.

## What it is
A No Objection Certificate confirming that the building's fire detection, suppression, escape, and access provisions meet the required standard for its occupancy class.

## Why it is required
Insurance claims, factory licensing, and occupancy certification all depend on it. More practically, it is the approval that dictates physical building design — staircase width, refuge areas, hydrant layout, water tank capacity — so discovering the requirement late is expensive.

## Two-stage process
1. **Provisional / plan-stage NOC** — building drawings are vetted for fire safety compliance before construction.
2. **Final NOC** — after installation, the fire service inspects the actual hydrants, sprinklers, alarms, extinguishers, escape routes, and static water storage.

## Typical documents required
- Building plans showing escape routes, staircases, and fire equipment layout
- Details of fire detection and suppression systems installed
- Static water storage and pump capacity details
- Building height, covered area, and occupancy classification
- Undertaking on maintenance of fire safety equipment
- Structural stability certificate in many cases

## Position in the approval sequence
Plan-stage approval runs alongside Building Plan Approval. Final NOC follows construction and precedes or accompanies the Factory License inspection.

## Practical notes
Requirements are driven by the National Building Code occupancy classification, so a warehouse, a food processing unit, and an office of identical floor area can face very different obligations. Periodic renewal and re-inspection apply.
