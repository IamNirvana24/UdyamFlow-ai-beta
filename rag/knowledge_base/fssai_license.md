# FSSAI Licence / Registration
slug: fssai_license
authority: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India, administered in Punjab by the Commissioner of Food Safety, via the FoSCoS portal
applies_to: Every business that manufactures, processes, packages, stores, transports, distributes, or sells food.

## What it is
Authorisation to operate a food business, issued at one of three tiers depending on scale and nature of operations.

## The three tiers
- **Basic Registration** — smallest food businesses, including petty manufacturers and small retailers.
- **State Licence** — mid-sized manufacturers, processors, and storage units operating within one state.
- **Central Licence** — large units above the prescribed capacity, importers, exporters, e-commerce food operators, and businesses operating across multiple states.

The tier is decided by annual turnover and by production capacity, and specific capacity limits apply to dairy, meat, and vegetable oil units regardless of turnover.

## Typical documents required
- Form B application signed by the proprietor or authorised signatory
- Layout plan of the processing unit showing area in square metres
- List of equipment and machinery with capacity
- List of food categories to be manufactured
- Water test report from an accredited laboratory, where water is used as an ingredient
- Food safety management plan
- Proof of premises possession and identity proof of the operator
- NOC from the local body and from the Pollution Board where applicable

## Position in the approval sequence
Sector-specific and typically obtained late in the sequence, once the premises is built and equipment installed, since the layout and equipment list are part of the application.

## Practical notes
Fees differ substantially across the three tiers and licences run for one to five years at the applicant's choice. The licence number must be displayed on the premises and printed on every food package. Misclassifying the tier is the most common cause of rejection.
