# Electricity Connection / NOC
slug: electricity_noc
authority: Punjab State Power Corporation Limited (PSPCL), with the Chief Electrical Inspector, Punjab, for installation safety approval
applies_to: All industrial and commercial premises requiring a power connection; the approval path depends on the sanctioned load and the supply voltage.

## What it is
Two related things: sanction and release of a power connection at a specified load by PSPCL, and safety approval of the internal electrical installation by the Electrical Inspectorate for higher-load or high-voltage installations.

## Why it is required
No connection means no production. Beyond that, the Electrical Inspector's approval is a precondition to energising a high-tension installation, and the Factory Inspectorate will ask for it.

## How load changes the path
Small loads are released on a straightforward low-tension connection. Above the prescribed load, supply moves to high tension, which brings in a dedicated substation, a transformer, metering at HT, and inspection of the installation by the Electrical Inspectorate before energisation.

## Typical documents required
- Application specifying connected load and demand
- Proof of premises ownership or occupancy
- Trade licence and where applicable Factory Licence
- Pollution Board consent, frequently asked for industrial connections
- Test report of the internal installation signed by a licensed electrical contractor
- Layout of the substation and earthing arrangement for HT connections
- Fire NOC for the substation where required

## Position in the approval sequence
Applied for mid-sequence, but because sanction and physical release can take weeks and may need infrastructure work, the application is usually filed well before the unit is ready.

## Practical notes
Security deposits and service connection charges scale with sanctioned load. Declaring a load lower than the actual requirement to reduce cost causes problems later, since exceeding sanctioned load attracts penalties and unauthorised extension is a serious violation.
