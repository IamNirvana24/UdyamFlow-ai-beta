# Building Plan Approval
slug: building_plan_approval
authority: Municipal Corporation, Improvement Trust, or the relevant Development Authority / PUDA depending on where the site falls; Department of Local Government, Punjab
applies_to: Any new construction, and material alteration or extension of existing structures, within the jurisdiction of a planning authority.

## What it is
Sanction of the building drawings — site plan, floor plans, elevations, and sections — confirming that the proposed construction conforms to the zoning, permitted land use, floor area ratio, setbacks, height limits, and parking norms for that plot.

## Why it is required
It is the first physical-works approval. Building without sanction risks demolition or compounding penalties, and an unsanctioned structure cannot get an occupancy certificate, which in turn blocks the Factory Licence and often the power connection.

## Typical documents required
- Title documents or lease deed for the plot
- Change of land use certificate where the land is not already zoned for the proposed use
- Site plan and building drawings prepared by a registered architect
- Structural drawings and stability certificate from a registered structural engineer
- Soil investigation report for larger structures
- Fire safety scheme for buildings above the prescribed height or area
- Undertaking regarding construction as per sanctioned plan

## Position in the approval sequence
The first approval in the chain wherever construction is involved. Everything physical depends on it.

## Practical notes
Land use is the make-or-break question. A plot zoned for residential or agricultural use cannot host an industrial unit without a change of land use, which is a separate and slower process. Checking the master plan zoning for the plot before purchase is the single highest-value piece of due diligence in this entire list. An occupancy or completion certificate is obtained after construction and is distinct from the plan sanction.
