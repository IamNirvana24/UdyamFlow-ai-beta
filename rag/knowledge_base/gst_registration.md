# GST Registration
slug: gst_registration
authority: Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) and Punjab State GST Department, via the GST portal
applies_to: Businesses crossing the prescribed aggregate turnover threshold, plus several categories where registration is mandatory regardless of turnover.

## What it is
Registration under the Goods and Services Tax regime, producing a 15-digit GSTIN tied to the PAN and the state of operation.

## Why it is required
Without a GSTIN a business cannot legally collect GST, cannot claim input tax credit on purchases, and cannot raise a compliant tax invoice. Most corporate buyers and e-commerce platforms will not onboard an unregistered supplier.

## When it is mandatory regardless of turnover
- Inter-state supply of goods
- Supply through an e-commerce operator
- Businesses liable to pay tax under reverse charge
- Casual taxable persons and non-resident taxable persons
- Input service distributors and agents supplying on behalf of others

## Typical documents required
- PAN of the business and of the promoters
- Aadhaar of the authorised signatory (used for e-KYC authentication)
- Proof of business registration or incorporation certificate
- Identity and address proof of promoters and directors with photographs
- Proof of principal place of business (ownership document, rent agreement, electricity bill)
- Bank account statement or cancelled cheque
- Digital signature where required by entity type

## Position in the approval sequence
Applied for early, alongside or just after the Trade License. It is a prerequisite for raising invoices, so it must be in place before commercial operations begin.

## Practical notes
There is no government fee for GST registration itself. Turnover thresholds differ between goods and services and have been revised over time, so the current threshold applicable to the specific sector and state must be verified on the GST portal before assuming registration is optional.
